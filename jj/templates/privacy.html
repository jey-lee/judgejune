Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At Judge Assist, we take the privacy of judges, debaters, coaches, and academic institutions seriously. This Privacy Policy details how we handle speech streams, debate metadata, and AI-assisted ballot generation.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the essential data necessary to deliver real-time speech transcription, flow tracking, and ballot reasoning:

  • Account Credentials: Username, email address, and encrypted passwords upon signup.
  • Debate Round Metadata: Event type (e.g., Public Forum, Parliamentary Debate), resolution text, speaker names, and speech flow notes created by the judge.
  • Ephemeral Audio Streams: Real-time microphone audio PCM chunks transmitted during active live transcription sessions.

2. Real-Time Speech Processing & Cartesia Ink 2 STT

Live speech transcription is powered by Cartesia Ink 2 low-latency WebSockets:

  • Audio buffers (16kHz PCM_S16LE) are processed strictly in ephemeral memory to generate word-by-word streaming text.
  • Zero Permanent Audio Retention: Raw microphone audio is never written to disk or used for training underlying speech models. Once the WebSocket turn ends, transient audio buffers are immediately discarded.

3. AI Ballot Generation & GPT-5.6 Reasoning

When you click "Generate Results", the text transcript and round flow are transmitted over TLS-encrypted APIs to GPT-5.6:

  • Prompts contain only text arguments, resolution details, and judging criteria.
  • No Model Training: Your round transcripts and debate ballots are processed under enterprise Zero-Data-Retention (ZDR) clauses and are not used to train AI models.

4. Data Security & Storage

All data in transit is protected using TLS 1.3 encryption, and stored databases enforce row-level session authorization so only authenticated round creators can access their sessions.

5. User Control & Instant Data Deletion

You maintain 100% control over your data. When you delete a round or session from your profile dashboard, all associated transcripts, results, and speaker point notes are permanently deleted from our database instantly.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

We work exclusively with vetted infrastructure providers who comply with high privacy standards:

  • Cartesia AI: Real-time WebSocket speech-to-text processing.
  • OpenAI: GPT-5.6 / o3-mini automated ballot reasoning.

7. Contact & Privacy Inquiries

If you have questions regarding this policy or data protection, please email our privacy team at privacy@judgeassist.com.
